## Detector comparison

_generated 2026-09-09 17:51_

30 photos, identical input to all three — the detector is the only variable. CPU only.

| detector | found a face | rate | avg per photo |
|---|---|---|---|
| opencv | 16/30 | 53% | 226ms |
| retinaface | 29/30 | 97% | 14390ms |
| insightface | 30/30 | 100% | 283ms |

**Embedding agreement** — retinaface vs insightface on the photos both found. Near 0 means the cheap detector hands the embedder the same face as the expensive one, so the speed win costs no accuracy:

| photos | count | min | median | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| real photos (one subject) | 2 | 0.0053 | 0.0055 | 0.0055 |
| synthetic composites (two faces) | 17 | 0.0517 | 0.1739 | 0.4152 |

The two rows measure different things. On a single-subject photo there is only one face to find, so this is genuine embedding agreement. The composites hold two faces and the detectors rank them differently — DeepFace returns its own first face, this pipeline takes the largest — so a high distance there often means they embedded *different people*, not that either was wrong.
